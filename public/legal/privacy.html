Pins Pets Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Pins Pets is published by 220 TECH LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy describes how the Pins Pets mobile and web apps handle information.

What Pins Pets stores on your device

Pet profiles, medication logs, inventory, and schedules stay on your device. They are encrypted locally (AES-256-GCM). We cannot read this data from our servers because it is not uploaded by default.

Account information

If you create an account, we store your email through our authentication provider (Supabase) so you can sign in. During onboarding we may ask for age range and gender for anonymous, aggregate product stats only. We do not sell this information.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers for ads
  • No analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal or pet-health data
  • Pet health records are not sent to us unless you later enable an explicit cloud backup feature

Third parties

You can use the tracker without an account. If you sign in, that may use Supabase and, if you choose them, Sign in with Apple or Google. Those providers process the email they return under their own policies.

Your choices

You can use Pins Pets without an account. If you sign in, you can sign out or permanently delete the account in the app: open Settings (the shield on Home) and choose Delete account. Deletion removes the account and the profile stored with it and cannot be undone. Pet logs stored only on the device are not uploaded; uninstall the app to remove them from the device. You can also email us for help.

Children

Pins Pets is not directed at children under 13.

Veterinary disclaimer

Pins Pets is a personal organization tool. It is not a veterinary device and does not provide veterinary advice. Always consult a qualified veterinarian.
